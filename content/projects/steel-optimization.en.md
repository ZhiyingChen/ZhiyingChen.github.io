## Overview

This series addresses raw-material and ironmaking optimization in steel production. It goes beyond calculating blend proportions to connect raw-material prices, grades, ignition losses, return fines, fuel consumption, slag properties, and hot-metal cost in one analytical framework.

I built three models for decisions at different levels: whole-process coordination, ore blending, and sintering.

## What problems does this series address?

- Raw-material decisions in steelmaking involve connected production stages.
- Prices, grades, losses, return fines, fuel consumption, slag properties, and hot-metal cost affect one another.
- Different decisions require models at different levels of detail.

## How the three projects fit together

- **Core project:** integrated sintering and ironmaking, minimizing total cost per tonne of hot metal.
- **Supporting project:** ore blending, focusing on the raw-material structure and cost balance at the blending stage.
- **Supporting project:** sintering, focusing on blend proportions and process constraints.

## Capabilities involved

- Abstracting complex operational problems.
- Modeling costs across production processes.
- Designing nonlinear optimization models and constraints.
- Delivering Excel-based and scripted analysis workflows.

## Applications

- Adjusting the raw-material mix at steel plants.
- Evaluating new ore sources.
- Coordinating sintering and ironmaking decisions.
- Comparing costs across multiple scenarios.
