My journey started with a simple question: how can mathematics help solve real-world problems?

Early work in operations research taught me to translate complex operational constraints into mathematical models. Working across manufacturing and logistics later showed me that a good optimization model is only part of the solution.

Useful results also require understanding business processes, working with imperfect data, designing practical systems, and making better decisions possible.

Today, I am interested in the intersection of operations research, manufacturing digitalization, and decision systems.
